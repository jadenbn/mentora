How to read every number on this page

One scale runs through the whole engine. A score rates one answered question from 0 to 1. Correct unassisted 1.0, correct with a hint 0.6, partial 0.3, wrong 0.0. That is score_attempt in engine/accuracy.py, and nothing else in the system is ever scored. Every other number on this page averages those four values over a topic's last 8 attempts.

Two averages, and the gap between them is the point.

  • Observed score. The raw mean, exactly what happened, or — before the first attempt. Read it, but note that the engine never decides on it. Two attempts and eight attempts read the same here.
  • Estimated score. The same mean pulled toward a 0.50 no-evidence prior, so it always has a value and one lucky answer cannot swing it. estimated_accuracy adds 2 pseudo-observations at 0.5, so a student's own record starts to outweigh the prior around their 3rd real attempt. Selection acts on this one. It sets the difficulty served next and feeds the weakness term in pick_topic. A wide gap from the observed score means the student has few attempts, nothing more.

Measure the Selection policy with a simulation

Replay pick_topic → mark_served → record_attempt loop against synthetic students in a throwaway in-memory database (DOES NOT TOUCH mentora.db), checks whether the selection policy itself is behaving:

  • does it cover the course instead of grinding a handful of topics?
  • holds difficulty steady rather than let it collapse?
  • avoids repeating the same topic back to back?
  • serves harder questions as a student's estimated score rises?

Profile lets you choose between student archetypes. Under the hood just changes two values of the profile (ability_range, Learning Rate) ability range is where their true skill in any given topic is set at. Learning Rate is how fast that student increases from that ability.

Coverage is the share of the course's topics the average student actually got. Observed score averages the first third of a run against the last third. It should stay level rather than climb, because the difficulty served rises with the estimated score. Difficulty does the same two thirds for the difficulty the engine asked for, 0 to 1, and Growth is that gap. Positive growth means the engine pushed students harder as they improved. Level score plus positive growth is the shape you want. Repeat rate is the share of picks that served the same topic twice in a row.

TopicServedObserved scoreEstimated score

The same two columns as the topics board above, across the whole cohort instead of one student. Served counts how many times anyone in the cohort got that topic. Observed score is the raw mean over those attempts. A — means nobody was ever served it, which we deliberately keep distinct from scoring 0. Estimated score is where the engine's belief lands after the run, averaged across the cohort. A student never served the topic counts at the flat 0.50 prior, exactly as a real never-attempted topic reads. That prior also parks any lightly-served topic near 0.50 whatever happened on it. Both columns report what the engine saw and believes, never the synthetic learner's hidden true ability.
